Gym Guidelines

Stay Safe, Respect the Space, Train Smart

These guidelines help everyone enjoy the gym safely and build positive habits.

1. Check In

Sign in at the gym desk and let staff know you are attending your session.

2. Warm Up Properly

Always perform dynamic warm-up drills before lifting or intense cardio.

3. Use Equipment Carefully

Use only the equipment approved for your program and ask for help if you are unsure.

4. Respect Others

Share space, keep noise at a respectful level, and follow staff instructions.

5. Stay Hydrated

Bring a water bottle and drink between sets to keep performance strong.
