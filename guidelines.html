Gym Guidelines for Every Member

To maintain a safe, respectful, and supportive training environment, all members must follow our gym guidelines. These rules ensure everyone can train effectively and comfortably.

Safety First

Use proper form and technique on all exercises. Always warm up before starting your workout, use appropriate weights for your fitness level, and never hesitate to ask coaches for form corrections.

Respect the Space

Return all equipment to its proper location after use. Wipe down machines and benches, keep noise levels reasonable, and maintain cleanliness throughout the facility.

Community Standards

Treat all members and staff with courtesy and respect. No discrimination, harassment, or bullying of any kind. Everyone deserves a welcoming and supportive environment.

Detailed Guidelines

Training Etiquette

  • Equipment Sharing: Don't hog equipment. When others are waiting, limit your rest time and complete your sets efficiently.
  • Bathrooms: Keep restrooms clean. Report any maintenance issues to staff immediately.
  • Spotting: Always ask before spotting someone, and ensure you're able to safely assist them.

Safety Requirements

  • Appropriate Footwear: Wear closed-toe athletic shoes with good grip for stability and protection.
  • Never Train Alone: Always have at least one other person in the gym. Never lift heavy weights without a spotter.
  • Form Over Ego: Lift weights that allow you to maintain proper form. Incorrect form leads to injury.
  • Know Your Limits: Progress gradually. Increase weight and intensity only when ready.
  • Hydration & Health: Bring water, stay hydrated, and avoid training if you're sick or injured.
  • Report Issues: Immediately report broken equipment, spills, or safety hazards to staff.

Facility Maintenance

  • Clean Up After Yourself: Wipe down all equipment, benches, and machines after use with provided towels.
  • Equipment Care: Handle all equipment with respect. No slamming, throwing, or misusing weights.
  • Lost & Found: Check the front desk for lost items. ASHS Gym is not responsible for lost or stolen belongings.

Behavior & Conduct

  • No Bullying or Harassment: Everyone belongs here. Support your fellow members, regardless of fitness level.
  • Prohibited Items: No weapons, illegal substances, or alcohol allowed on premises.

Membership Rules

  • Access Hours: The gym is open during posted hours. Members must exit by closing time.

Trial Access

Interested in trying out ASHS Gym before committing to a membership? We'd love to have you! To arrange a trial session or visit, please talk to the Sports Department. They'll help you get started and answer any questions about joining our community.

Membership Cost: Only $50 for the whole year! Join now and become part of the ASHS Gym community.

Violations & Enforcement

Failure to follow these guidelines may result in:

  • First Violation: Verbal warning
  • Second Violation: Written warning
  • Third Violation: Temporary suspension
  • Serious Violations: Immediate termination of membership

Serious violations include dangerous behavior, harassment, theft, or any illegal activity.

Questions? We're Here to Help
