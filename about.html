About Our Gym

Welcome to ASHS Gym

ASHS Gym is the school’s dedicated fitness space for students who want to improve their physical fitness, learn correct exercise form, and develop a routine that works for them.

Our Mission

To create an inclusive environment where every student can access safe training, learn fitness fundamentals, and grow stronger with confidence.

What We Offer

Workout plans, supervised sessions, guided warm-ups, cool-downs, and flexible programming for different skill levels.

Student Success

Through consistency, teamwork, and positive coaching, members build healthier habits that last beyond the school year.
