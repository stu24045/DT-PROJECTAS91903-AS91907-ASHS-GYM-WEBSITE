FAQs & Progress Tips

How do I progress?

Increase load or reps gradually each week. Aim for +1–2 reps or +2.5–5kg when a top set is achievable with good form.

How much rest?

Strength sets: 2–4 minutes. Hypertrophy: 60–90s. Conditioning: vary by modality.

Recovery

Prioritise 7–9 hours sleep, good nutrition, and active recovery on rest days.
