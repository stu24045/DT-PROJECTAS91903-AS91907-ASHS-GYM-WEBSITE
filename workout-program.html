Workout Program

Programs for Every Student

Our workout program includes strength training, cardio conditioning, flexibility work, and recovery routines that are easy to follow and school-safe.

Beginner Circuit

Perfect for new members. Focus on bodyweight strength, movement patterns, and building confidence.

  • Warm-up
  • Sit-ups, push-ups, squats
  • Light cardio
  • Stretching

Strength Sessions

For students ready to lift heavier with supervision, using free weights and resistance machines safely.

  • Squat variations
  • Presses and pulls
  • Core stability
  • Technique focus

Active Recovery

Keep muscles healthy with mobility drills, stretching, and low-impact movement to recover smartly.

  • Foam rolling
  • Dynamic stretches
  • Breathing practice
  • Hydration tips

Pick the program that fits your goals

Whether you want to build toning, improve endurance, or start a consistent weekly routine, ASHS Gym has a plan for you.
